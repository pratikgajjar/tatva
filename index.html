Who We Are

About

Tatva Systems is founded by Pratik Gajjar, a software engineer passionate about tackling both technical challenges and everyday problems.

With expertise spanning backend development, database systems, and distributed architectures, we focus on finding solutions and making improvements where possible, whether that means optimizing existing systems or building new ones from the ground up.

The name "Tatva" (तत्व) comes from Sanskrit, meaning "essence" or "fundamental principle", reflecting our approach to engineering: understanding the core of problems before crafting solutions.
